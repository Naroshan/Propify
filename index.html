How it works

Pick your move. We’ll show you every step.

  1. 01
    Get a mortgage in principleA quick check of how much a lender is likely to lend you. Agents take your offer more seriously with one.
    1 dayWe arrange: Mortgage
  2. 02
    View homesBook viewings with the agent. Take photos and ask about the bills, the neighbours and why they’re selling.
    Your pace
  3. 03
    Make an offerOnce it’s accepted, the agent tells everyone involved and the home should come off the market.
    1–3 days
  4. 04
    Instruct a solicitorThey do the legal checks and move the money safely. This is also called conveyancing.
    8–12 weeksWe arrange: Conveyancing
  5. 05
    Get a surveyAn expert checks the condition of the home, so you know about problems before you commit.
    1–2 weeksWe arrange: Survey
  6. 06
    Renegotiate if neededIf the survey finds issues, you can ask for a lower price or for repairs. We’ll help you word it.
    If needed
  7. 07
    Exchange contractsThe deal becomes legally binding and you pay your deposit. The moving date is now fixed.
    1 day
  8. 08
    Complete and get the keysThe money moves, the home is yours, and the agent hands over the keys.
    Moving day
