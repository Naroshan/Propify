Privacy notice

Your details, your say.

What we collect, why, and who sees it — in plain English.

What we collect

When you build a plan or contact us, we collect the details you give us: your name, email address, phone number (if you add one), the property location (if you add one), what you’re doing with your property and your answers to our questions.

Why we collect it

  • To build your plan and show you relevant services and guide prices.
  • With your consent, to introduce you to the specialists for the services you choose, so they can contact you with quotes.
  • To reply when you contact us.
  • If you opt in, to send occasional tips and updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Who we share it with

Only the specialists providing the services you’ve chosen — for example a conveyancer, an FCA-authorised mortgage adviser, a RICS-regulated surveyor, an auction house or a removals firm. We never sell your data.

How your details reach us

When you send a plan, an enquiry or a “notify me” request, it is delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), an email-forwarding service. It is used only to pass your details to us.

What’s stored on your device

Your plan (its reference, the services you chose, your first name and email) is saved in your browser so you can come back to it. You can remove it at any time by starting a new plan or clearing your browser data.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent. Email Mr.w.davey@hotmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Talk to a real person

Not sure where to start? Tell us what’s going on and someone from the Properfy team will point you in the right direction. We serve customers in England and Wales only.
