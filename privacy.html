Privacy notice

Your details, your say.

What we collect, why, and who sees it, in plain English.

What we collect

When you start a move, we collect what you tell us: what you’re doing, the property’s address, postcode and price, whether you want us to introduce an estate agent, mortgage broker or solicitor, and your name, email and phone number. When you ask us to arrange a service, we note which one. If you’re a partner registering interest, we collect your name, firm, role, office postcode and contact details.

Why we collect it

  • To set up your move and show you the steps involved.
  • With your consent, to introduce you to specialists and arrange the services you ask for.
  • To reply when you contact us.

Who we share it with

Only the specialists you ask us to introduce or arrange, such as a solicitor or conveyancer, a mortgage adviser, a surveyor or an auction house. We never sell your data.

How your details reach us

Forms on this site are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), an email-forwarding service used only to pass your details to us.

What’s stored on your device

Your move tracker is saved in your browser so you can come back to it. You can remove it at any time from the “My move” page, or by clearing your browser data.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent. Email Mr.w.davey@hotmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).
