Questions

How long does a transfer of equity take?

Often a few weeks to a couple of months, mostly depending on lender approval.

Do I pay Stamp Duty?

Sometimes, if someone takes on mortgage debt or pays money. Some situations are exempt.

Does my lender need to agree?

Yes, if there’s a mortgage on the property.
